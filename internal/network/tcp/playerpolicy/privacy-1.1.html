EQOA Classic Privacy Notice

Version 1.1 · November 24, 2026

The people who operate EQOA Classic use player information to run the website and game servers, restore access to accounts, investigate bugs and moderate the community. For a private question or request, use Join our Discord on the project homepage and ask to speak privately with an operator. You can use this route even if you cannot sign in to the game. Accounts and game progress

  • Signing in. The account system uses your account name and ID, a password verification record, creation and login times, and account status. We receive your connection's IP address and use it for purposes such as limiting registrations and repeated sign-in attempts. Connection details can also appear in operational logs.
  • Email and recovery. We use your email address and verification status to verify the account and help you recover it. The normal GM email screen masks the address. Our mail service processes the address to deliver verification and recovery messages. If a GM helps you recover access, we also receive the contact or character details you choose to provide. Account help
  • Two servers. Heirloom and Live share account information, including account bans. Each keeps its own characters, progress, guilds, mail, banks and petitions. Other players can see information the game displays, such as character names, guild membership and entries on public leaderboards. Server guide
  • Your device. The account pages use a sign-in cookie. The account website signs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity. The browser client reads the disc image on your device. If you choose Remember my disc images, the browser remembers where to find them. The disc image stays on your device. Browser client
  • Agreement. The agreement page uses your age to check eligibility and keeps the outcome in a temporary browser cookie. An eligible result is cleared when you finish the form or expires after 30 minutes. An ineligible result is kept in this browser for up to 24 hours to discourage an immediate retry. We do not store your exact age or birth date from this form. When you accept the Player Terms, we record your account ID, the terms version and the acceptance time so we can establish which terms you agreed to.

Chat and moderation

The game server can write the text of say, shout, group and guild messages to its logs. For tells, the normal delivery log records the sender and recipient. When detailed network logging is enabled for troubleshooting, its logs can also contain in-game message text, including tells. An authorized GM monitoring a character can see the messages that character sends in real time, including tells. Recent chat shown to a character can also be included in a support report. Take this into account when deciding what to share, even in a tell or a small group.

Staff use game, connection and moderation records to investigate problems and enforce community decisions. The project keeps records of moderation actions such as account bans or chat mutes. GM panel changes are audited and can include the affected account, the staff action and a recorded reason.

Support, transfers and crash reports

An in-game petition, /bug or /feature report can include your character and account identifiers, your text and replies, and a snapshot of what was happening in the game. That snapshot can include the character's location in the game, target, nearby entities and recently displayed messages. The bug-finder leaderboard can credit your character by name.

After a crash, a supported project client can automatically send a diagnostic report at the next login. It can include the client build, platform, graphics details and error trace. The server files it as a support ticket for the character you sign in with, so staff can associate the report with that character.

If you request a transfer, GMs review the old server and character details, progress, equipment, notes and any screenshot link you submit. Use your EQOA Classic credentials on our sign-in page and keep the other server's credentials to yourself. The map and spell-visual suggestion forms also use a hashed form of the sender's IP address to limit duplicate or excessive submissions. Transfer page

Who can receive information

Players receive the information you share with them through game features. Authorized project staff use account, game and support records to run the service. Hosting and mail providers process information needed to operate their services. Staff also use connected AI tools to investigate player reports and develop fixes. The providers of those tools can process the ticket text, character identifiers or diagnostic details staff use for that work. We may disclose records when the law requires it.

If you use Discord to contact us, Discord processes what you send there under its own privacy policy. If you submit a link to a screenshot, its host also handles access to that image. Share only the details needed for the request. Discord Privacy Policy

How long we keep information

When we decide how long to keep information, we consider whether we still need it to run your account, enforce or review a GM decision, investigate a problem, recover the service or meet a legal obligation. We cannot promise a fixed deletion date for every log or backup.

  • Your account and characters. We keep account information and saved progress so you can sign back in, recover access and continue playing. Signing out leaves those records in place. An agreement record lets us establish which version of the Player Terms you accepted if a question about your account arises.
  • GM decisions. An ongoing suspension can require us to keep the account identifier, the action, when it happened and the reason for as long as the suspension is in force. A report or a relevant chat excerpt may be needed to investigate repeated abuse or reconsider a decision. An audit entry may remain after a suspension ends so operators can review what staff did or address a dispute.
  • Chat, bugs and crashes. We use chat and connection logs, tickets and crash reports to diagnose problems, check fixes and investigate security or abuse reports. A report linked to a character is player information even if it contains only technical details. A closed petition leaves your in-game inbox after seven days. Staff can still retrieve closed tickets and tickets you remove from your inbox. Resolving a bug or closing a ticket does not automatically erase the underlying records.
  • Backups. We keep database copies to recover from a failure or a bad change. The automatic process that backs up a shard before certain database changes keeps up to 20 copies of that type per shard. Manual backups and copies held by the cloud host can last longer. Removing information from the running game can leave an earlier copy in a backup until that backup is retired.

We may keep anonymous service statistics, such as total crashes by client version, for longer when they cannot reasonably be linked back to a player. A character name, account ID, IP address or distinctive chat text can still connect a game or troubleshooting record to someone.

Players under 13

EQOA Classic accounts and gameplay are for people age 13 and older. Any age question at signup or sign-in is used to check eligibility. If we learn that a player is under 13, we disable access, stop further collection through that account and promptly begin the process of deleting the child's personal information, including contacting providers that hold it for us as necessary. A parent or guardian can report an account privately to an operator through the official Discord linked on our homepage. We use the details in that report to locate the account and handle the request. Contact the operators

Your requests

You can ask what information we hold about your account or ask us to correct or delete it. Use Join our Discord on the homepage and ask to speak privately with an operator. You can do this while suspended or signed out. We may need to verify your connection to the account. We will explain what we can remove and any limits on the request. For example, applicable law may allow or require us to keep a specific record to enforce an active ban, resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation. Your rights under applicable privacy law still apply. EQOA Classic homepage
